\section{Research Interests}
My doctoral research, which I conceived and proposed, spans two complementary directions:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Quantum hardware security.} Characterizing crosstalk at the pulse level on shared superconducting processors, distinguishing interference from calibration drift, and verifying compiled quantum workloads across hardware and circuit layers.
  \item \textbf{Quantum communication and quantum correlations.} Designing adaptive protocols for error correction and purification, and using optimal POVMs to transmit states of multiple qubits over partially entangled GHZ channels. Applications include quantum repeaters and the quantum internet.
\end{itemize}
Related interests include quantum cryptography, quantum error correction, quantum algorithms, and machine learning for quantum characterization.

\section{Selected Research Projects and Software}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Superconducting Qubit Simulation and Hardware Modeling} \,(\cvlink{https://github.com/syedshubha/EmulateQPU-4Q}{GitHub}).
        Built a Python simulator for four transmons and three tunable couplers with programmable microwave and flux controls. Designed layouts with shared and dedicated readout feedlines, Purcell filters, and a carrier with wire bonds. Generated Gmsh meshes and Palace field inputs and published a \cvlink{https://github.com/syedshubha/EmulateQPU-4Q/tree/main/reproducibility/mesh-ladder}{comparison across three meshes} for capacitance and charging energy. Added regression tests for sampled controls. On one Slurm node, five circuit tasks matched serial results exactly and an interrupted worker recovered on retry.
  \item \textbf{Interactive Quantum Singular Value Transformation Laboratory} \,(\cvlink{https://syedshubha.com/qsvt/}{web demo}). Built a browser tool that constructs unitary block encodings of rectangular matrices, fits phases for quantum signal processing, and checks polynomial responses against independent numerical samples.
  \item \textbf{Neural Shadows: ML for Entanglement Classification} \,(\cvlink{https://github.com/syedshubha/ShadowTomography}{GitHub}).
        Classified entanglement of Werner states from 25 classical-shadow snapshots per state. Benchmarked logistic regression, random forest, FNN, and RBF-SVM classifiers over 100 Monte Carlo trials (mean F1 0.80--0.82).
  \item \textbf{W-State Preparation on Superconducting Qubits with Qiskit Pulse} \,(\cvlink{https://github.com/syedshubha/w-state-qiskit-pulse}{GitHub}).
        Built Gaussian single-qubit, echoed cross-resonance CNOT, and measurement pulse schedules for a three-qubit W state from \textit{ibm\_sherbrooke} calibration data, with $\pi$-pulse amplitudes from noisy simulated Rabi sweeps.
  \item \textbf{Quantum Image Encoding and Decoding} \,(\cvlink{https://github.com/syedshubha/QuantumImageProcessing/blob/main/NEQR_FRQI.ipynb}{GitHub}).
        Implemented FRQI and NEQR encodings in Qiskit, generalized both encoding circuits, and developed a decoding method to reconstruct output images.
  \item \textbf{Load Flow Analysis for $n$-Bus Power Systems} \,(\cvlink{https://github.com/syedshubha/load_flow_analysis_gauss_seidel}{GitHub}).
        Generalized a Gauss--Seidel solver with an adjustable convergence tolerance.
\end{itemize}
